The [Walshit status page](https://status.walshit.com/) is now live. It gives
you a quick view of current service health across the five main public doors,
all in one place.

Alongside current status, it shows measured availability and any incident or
maintenance notes. Those measurements describe what the page has observed;
they aren't an uptime guarantee.

Active incidents belong on the status page. Broader service notes and planned
changes will continue here in Updates.
